# Customer-Engagement-Success-Analysis-for-Restraurant-
#📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes how user engagement metrics influence restaurant success using Yelp data.
The goal is to understand whether reviews, tips, check-ins, sentiment, and elite user activity contribute to higher ratings and sustained business growth.

The analysis leverages SQL, Python, and statistical techniques to uncover data-driven business insights.

🎯 Business Problem
In a competitive restaurant industry, understanding what drives customer engagement and business success is crucial.

This project investigates:

Does higher engagement lead to better ratings?
Do reviews, tips, and check-ins correlate with each other?
Do elite users influence restaurant visibility?
How does engagement vary across cities and time?
What are the peak engagement hours?
📂 Dataset Overview
Source: Yelp Dataset (JSON format)
150K+ businesses analyzed
35K active restaurants selected
Data stored in SQL database for structured querying
Tables used:
Business
Review
User
Tip
Check-in
🛠 Tech Stack
SQL – Data extraction & aggregation
Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn) – EDA & visualization
Statistical Analysis – Hypothesis testing & correlation analysis
Jupyter Notebook – Analysis workflow
Database Creation Notebook – Structured data storage
🔍 Key Findings
1️⃣ Engagement vs Ratings
Engagement increases from 1⭐ to 4⭐ restaurants
4-star restaurants show the highest engagement
5-star restaurants show slight engagement drop (possible saturation effect)
👉 Insight: Extremely high ratings do not always mean highest engagement.

2️⃣ Correlation Between Engagement Metrics
Strong positive correlation observed between:

Review Count
Tip Count
Check-in Count
👉 Improving one engagement metric likely increases others.

3️⃣ High-Rated vs Low-Rated Businesses
High-rated restaurants:

Higher review count
Higher check-ins
More tips
👉 Higher ratings are associated with sustained and multi-channel engagement.

4️⃣ Geographic Insights
Top-performing city:

Philadelphia
Other strong markets:

Tampa
Indianapolis
Tucson
👉 Location significantly impacts restaurant success.

5️⃣ Time Trend Analysis
COVID-related engagement drop observed
High-rated restaurants recovered faster
Consistent engagement over time correlates with sustained success
6️⃣ Sentiment Metrics (Useful, Funny, Cool)
Higher counts of:

Useful
Funny
Cool reviews
→ Correlate positively with restaurant success score

👉 Quality of engagement matters, not just quantity.

7️⃣ Elite User Impact
Elite users are fewer in number
Contribute disproportionately high review volume
👉 Engaging elite users can amplify visibility and customer acquisition.

8️⃣ Busiest Hours
Peak engagement time: 🕓 4 PM – 1 AM

👉 Restaurants should optimize staffing and operations during these hours.

📈 Business Recommendations
Focus on maintaining 4+ star rating to maximize engagement.
Encourage reviews, check-ins, and tips simultaneously.
Build relationships with elite users for amplified reach.
Optimize staffing during evening peak hours.
Invest in markets like Philadelphia for expansion opportunities.
Monitor engagement trends to maintain long-term growth stability.
🚀 Conclusion
Restaurant success is not determined solely by ratings.

Sustained, multi-dimensional user engagement — including reviews, tips, check-ins, sentiment quality, and elite user participation — plays a critical role in long-term business performance.

Businesses that actively manage and optimize engagement channels are more likely to achieve sustainable growth and competitive advantage.
